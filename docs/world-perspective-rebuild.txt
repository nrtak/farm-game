World perspective rebuild — 2026-10-17

Direction
Keep the approved compact, aligned town blocks and extend the elevated angled presentation to the farm, harbor, shrine, tea country, highlands, waterfall, connecting road and interiors. Prefer clear roads and identifiable entrances over decorative obstacles.

User-provided general gameplay reference:
https://harvestmoon.fandom.com/wiki/Harvest_Moon:_Back_To_Nature
Use its farming routines, village life, festivals and progression as design reference. Tea Blossom Hills retains its own characters, art, locations, dialogue and Year 2 village-vote story.

Implementation
WorldPerspective projects the existing logical ground coordinates and follows the farmer. Touch destinations unproject through the viewport; joystick directions are converted into the ground plane. Character and building sprites remain upright. The existing town keeps its established projection. Save positions, quest destinations, collisions and service locations retain their logical coordinates.

WorldScenery replaces the old single painted backgrounds with projected ground, roads, water boundaries, regional landmark sprites and distinct room furnishings. Scenery uses the existing blocked footprints. Indoor furniture uses the collision rectangles. Calendar, storage, mining depletion, barn occupants, greenhouse crops, home expansions and seasonal coloring remain represented.

Deep review and fixes
Restored fishing feedback and placed both fishing floats in water rather than on the pier. Anchored pickup icons above the farmer in each projection. Restored the home weather television. Separated farm tool buttons from the message panel at desktop and small-phone viewport sizes. Corrected NPC facing and actor depth; excluded wildlife from roads and crop beds. Added visible woodland at blocked shrine boundaries and clipped atlas cells to avoid neighboring artwork leaking into props.
